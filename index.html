Gold news

Weekly drop as dollar and yields climb.
Gold slipped on 2 Oct and was heading for a weekly loss, with a firm US dollar and high Treasury yields as headwinds. Source: Trading Economics
Fed hike hurt September.
The Fed raised rates by 0.25 points on 16 Sep, to 3.75-4.00%, and gold had its worst month since June. Source: Discovery Alert
Hike bets eased after softer inflation.
Weaker August inflation cut the chance of an October hike to roughly 28%, but the 10-year US yield is at a 20-year high. Source: CNBC, 1 Oct
HSBC lowers its forecast, sees a bottom near.
It cut 2026 and 2027 average forecasts to $4,490 and $4,825, saying gold may be close to a low. Source: CNBC
Chart watchers see key support near $3,965-4,040.
The short-term trend is still called bearish; January's record close was near $5,405. Sources: Popeye's Chartbook, Canadian Mining Report

Silver news

Weekly decline, near two-month lows.
Silver is about 10% lower over the month but still about 26% above a year ago. Source: Trading Economics
Silver follows interest rates.
It rose 1.25% on 1 Oct as yields eased and was down about 0.2% on 2 Oct. It swings more than gold. Source: Rio Times, FXEmpire
Support near $60.8; a break could open $54.6.
Analysts watch whether silver holds its trendline. If the dollar and yields keep rising, a slide toward $54.6 is possible. Source: Coingabbar
Forecasts are all over the place.
UBS maps $70 by December. Other sites range from about $54 to $80 or more, so treat them with caution. Source: Canadian Mining Report

What could push prices up or down

Could push higher
Softer US jobs or inflation data, a Fed hold, lower Treasury yields, a weaker dollar, central bank or ETF buying, a weaker rupee.
Could push lower
Hot inflation, another Fed hike, yields above 5.3%, a stronger dollar, a stronger rupee, falling risk appetite for silver.
Dates to watch
US jobs and inflation reports before the Fed meeting on 27-28 October. The market is still pricing a likely hike in December.
